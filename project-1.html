A Platform for Career Exploration

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Cosmo Connect, a launchpad for career guidance and mentorship. It’s built for those with limited access to career support and helps them move forward with confidence.

TIMELINE

  1. Research: user interviews and competitive analysis.
  2. Ideation: wireframing and information architecture mapping.
  3. Prototyping: first drafts of lofi and midfi prototypes.
  4. Revisions: refined based on user testing feedback.
  5. Final Hifi:demonstration of mobile app.

BACKGROUND

Have you faced this challenge before?

As college students, we experienced the struggles of navigating the right career path. This empathy helped us connect with our target users, adolescents from 12-19 years old. They often face even greater challenges due to lack of guidance and preparation.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Ages 12-19 need greater exposure to niche career resources and practice in navigating a highly competitive job market.

SOLUTION

From Generic to Genuine: Advice That Fits You

Based on user feedback, we focused on making the career journey feel less abstract and more personalized, with bite-sized milestones, self-discovery tools, and mentorship access.
